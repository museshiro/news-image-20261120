# World News — Friday 20 November 2026

One image summing up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026, built for the
[make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "Sum up 20 November's world news in one image".

## The day in one image

`entry.png` is a newspaper front page for 20 November 2026:

- **Lead story — Anthropic soars on Wall Street debut.** The first frontier AI firm
  goes public, its shares jumping on the first day of trading.
- **North Korea fires ballistic missile.** South Korea reports a launch towards the
  Sea of Japan.
- **Kyiv hit by ballistic missiles.** Russian strikes hit the Ukrainian capital.
- **UK pledges 1,000 drones for Ukraine.** A £260 million package of long-range
  drones and capabilities.
- **Asian markets plunge on AI fears.** Taiwan stocks fall sharply amid AI bubble
  worries.
- **Trump pardons Pete Rose.** A posthumous pardon for the late baseball star.
- **COP31: Africa seeks climate finance.** African nations push for tripled climate
  funding in Antalya.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output
directory the grader provides. It runs with no network, and both runs write the
same bytes.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
